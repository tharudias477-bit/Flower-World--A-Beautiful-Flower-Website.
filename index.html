About Flower World

Our passion for fresh florals and memorable moments

Sri Lanka's Premier Boutique Florist


At Flower World, we source fresh flowers daily to create artistic bouquets. We bring emotion, luxury, and fragrance together for every special day across Sri Lanka.Founded with a passion for floral elegance, Flower World has grown into one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted destinations for premium floral design. Our team of expert florists carefully hand-crafts each arrangement, combining colors, textures, and seasonal blooms to convey your exact heart’s message. Whether it is a grand celebration or a simple gesture of love, we take pride in delivering perfection to your doorstep.

Beyond traditional bouquets, we are dedicated to creating complete memorable experiences. From curated gourmet gift hampers and artisanal treats to grand wedding floral setups, we blend creativity with reliable island-wide delivery. At Flower World, every petal tells a story, and we are honored to be a part of your life's most precious moments.
